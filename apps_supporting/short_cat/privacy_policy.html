Privacy Policy for Short Cat

Last updated: February 6, 2025

Introduction

Short Cat ("we," "our," or "the app") is a productivity Android app developed by Short Cat that creates customizable floating panel overlays with app shortcuts. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

Package name: com.vitaz.shortcat

Information We Collect

Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Short Cat stores the following data locally on your device only using a local database:

  • Panel configurations: Names, positions, layouts, and settings for your floating panels
  • App shortcuts: Package names of apps you add to panels and their order
  • Customization preferences: Panel colors, sizes, opacity, orientation, border colors
  • App preferences: Onboarding completion status, user preferences

Information Collected Automatically

We use third-party services that collect certain information to help us improve the app:

Google Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with our app. This service collects:

  • Usage events, including:
    • Onboarding progress (started, completed, skipped)
    • Feature usage (panels created/deleted, icons added/removed)
    • Overlay sessions (when opened/closed, duration)
    • Paywall interactions (views, dismissals)
    • Limit reached events (panel limits, icon limits)
  • Device information: Device model, operating system version, screen size
  • General location: Country/region level only (not precise location)
  • App performance data: Load times, responsiveness

We also set the following user properties:

  • Subscription status (free or premium)
  • Number of panels created

This data is collected in aggregate form and is used solely to improve the app experience. It does not personally identify you. Learn more about Google's privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports and diagnose technical issues. This includes:

  • Crash logs and stack traces
  • Device state at time of crash (memory, battery, network status)
  • Device model and operating system version
  • Custom diagnostic keys: subscription status, panel count
  • Anonymous user identifier (randomly generated UUID)

This information helps us identify and fix bugs to improve app stability. Crash data does not include any personal information.

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and in-app purchases for "Short Cat Pro." When you make a purchase or restore a subscription, RevenueCat processes:

  • Transaction data (purchase date, subscription status, expiration date, price)
  • Anonymous app user ID
  • Device identifiers for purchase validation
  • Platform (Android/Google Play)

RevenueCat does not have access to your payment details such as credit card numbers or billing address — these are handled securely by Google Play. You can review RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Permissions We Request

Short Cat requires several permissions to function. Here's why we need each one:

Permission Purpose
Display Over Other Apps
(SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)		 Core functionality — allows floating panels to appear on top of other applications. We do not use this to display ads or collect information from other apps.
Usage Access
(PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)		 Enables the "active app indicator" feature that highlights the currently running app in your panel with a colored border. We do not store, transmit, or share your app usage history.
Foreground Service
(FOREGROUND_SERVICE)		 Keeps the overlay panels running reliably while you use other apps. Required for the service notification.
Foreground Service Special Use
(FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE)		 Android 12+ requirement for running foreground services with overlay functionality.
Notifications
(POST_NOTIFICATIONS)		 Shows a persistent notification while panels are active, with controls to show, hide, or stop the overlay. Required on Android 13+.
Vibrate
(VIBRATE)		 Provides haptic feedback when interacting with panels (dragging, tapping icons).
Receive Boot Completed
(RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)		 Reserved for future functionality to optionally auto-start panels when your device boots. Not currently active.

How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app's core functionality
  • Process and manage your subscription purchases
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve features and user experience
  • Diagnose crashes, errors, and technical issues
  • Understand which features are most valuable to users
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information.

We only share data with the following third-party services, which are necessary for app functionality:

Service Purpose Data Shared
Google Firebase Analytics Usage analytics Anonymized usage events, device info
Firebase Crashlytics Crash reporting Crash logs, device info, anonymous ID
RevenueCat Subscription management Transaction data, anonymous user ID
Google Play Payment processing Handled by Google, not accessible to us

Data Retention

  • Local data: Stored on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data
  • Analytics data: Retained for 14 months per Google Firebase's default retention policy
  • Crash reports: Retained for 90 days
  • Subscription data: Retained by RevenueCat as long as necessary to provide subscription services and handle refunds/disputes

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect the information we collect:

  • All local data is stored in your device's private app storage, inaccessible to other apps
  • All network communications with third-party services use encrypted HTTPS connections
  • We do not operate our own servers — all data processing is handled by established, trusted third-party services

Your Rights and Choices

Opt-out of Analytics: You can limit analytics collection through your device settings:

  • Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID

Delete Local Data: Clear all local app data:

  • Settings > Apps > Short Cat > Storage > Clear Data

Manage Subscriptions: View and cancel subscriptions:

  • Google Play Store > Menu > Subscriptions

Uninstall: Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data immediately.

Request Data Deletion: Contact us at the email below to request deletion of any data associated with your anonymous identifier.

Children's Privacy

Short Cat is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is rated for general audiences and contains no age-restricted content.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the app in a way that provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

International Data Transfers

The third-party services we use (Google Firebase, RevenueCat) may process data in the United States or other countries. By using Short Cat, you consent to the transfer of anonymized usage data to these services in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy
  • For significant changes, provide notice through the app or app store listing

We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
